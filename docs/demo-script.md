# Demo Script (target: under 3 minutes)

1. **Open on the map** (10s) — full India view, colored dots already
   showing risk. Say: "Show us your highway, we'll tell you where animals
   will die next."
2. **Zoom to Bandipur/NH-766** (15s) — point at the cluster of rust-colored
   (high-risk) hotspots. Mention the night-ban dispute as the anchor story.
3. **Click a hotspot → recommendation card** (25s) — walk through risk
   score, confidence, species mix, seasonal curve, and the concrete
   intervention ("wildlife crossing, here, this exact spot").
4. **Show the network read-out strip** (10s) — the seismograph-style bar
   along the bottom; explain it's every hotspot nationwide, one continuous
   reading.
5. **Filter by species / endangered-only** (15s) — show the filter panel
   narrowing the map live.
6. **Report a sighting** (20s) — fill the form, submit, mention this
   directly feeds tomorrow's honesty-ladder count.
7. **Methodology page** (25s) — show the 92/150 honesty-ladder stat and
   explain why the model is secondary, not headline. This is the
   credibility beat — judges should leave trusting the number.
8. **Attribution page** (10s) — quick pass, zero paid services claim.
9. **Close on the anchor story** (10s) — "Both sides argue blind. Here's
   the evidence."
